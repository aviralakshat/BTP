# Papers to download

Save every file at the repository root, named `Title_Author.pdf`, matching the files already there. Text extraction happens after the PDFs are in the repo. Do not use a number from this list in the model until it has been read from the PDF.

Three new retrofits this batch is for:

1. **Feed and air preheating** (process-heat recovery, the alternative to WHR electricity).
2. **Producer gas** replacing the coal injected at the discharge end (the syngas case in the project brief).
3. **Oxygen enrichment** of kiln air.

A fourth, smaller case, **kiln air and coal-split control**, comes from the CSE report below.

## Already in the repo. Do not download again

Dey et al. 2015; Nduagu et al.; Nitturu / CEEW (both copies); Bhardwaj et al.; TERI energy-efficient technology options; BEE MSME guidelines; Hall et al.; Fan and Friedmann; Vigneswaran et al.; Gaurav et al. waste-heat recovery; Kamal et al.
