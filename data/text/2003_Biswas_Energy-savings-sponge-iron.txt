transfer mechanism inside a rotary kiln allows us to accurately
estimate Qs . The mechanism of heat transfer 关7兴 is very complex

a rotating kiln. Apart from the conduction through kiln wall, the
entire heat transfer involves five following components 共Fig. 3兲 as

  • Exposed wall-to-solid by radiation (Qwes)
  • Covered wall-to-solid by radiation and convection (Qwes)
  • Surface-to-interior of the bed by bulk mixing through con-

of various sizes of sponge iron plants. Estimation of heating up
cycle and cooling down period is not considered. Also, in the heat
transfer analysis, the estimation of heat transfer from the bed sur-
face to the interior of the bed by bulk mixing through tumbling
action is not considered, as appropriate values of shape factor
