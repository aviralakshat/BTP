      Beneﬁts of oxygen enrichment in sponge iron
      rotary kiln emerging from data-driven
      modelling
      N. Rajesh∗ 1,2, A. Nandi3 and S. K. Pabi1
      Optimal oxygen enrichment conditions for sponge iron rotary kiln have been successfully explored
      on an industrial scale using a data-driven model. A multi-objective optimisation by genetic
      algorithm (MOGA) is employed to ﬁnd the favourable conditions. The objective function for
      MOGA is derived from neural networks using pre-processed operational data. From industrial
      experimentations guided by the optimum conditions predicted by the present model, it emerged
      that when the coal ﬁnes injection is maintained at 1.75 tph and the oxygen enrichment is 8 Nm3 t−1
      of sponge iron, a reduction in the speciﬁc air requirement from 2609 to 2150 Nm3 t−1 was
      obtained, while the end-zone bed temperature remained under control at 1132°C. These
      conditions resulted in a reduction of speciﬁc coal consumption by 6%, an enhancement in the
      sponge iron production by 6% and an increase in the rotary kiln campaign life from 50 to 100 days.
      Keywords: Sponge iron rotary kiln, Oxygen enrichment, Data-driven model, Optimisation, Genetic algorithm, Artiﬁcial neural network
